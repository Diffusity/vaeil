# Free hosting verification

Checked against live official pages on **24 October 2026**. Prices are USD. This report supersedes the paid recommendation in `hosting-options-2026-10-24.md` for the user's new **$0 hosting budget**. No resources were created or purchased.

## Proposed topology

The proposed combination remains available: one Render Free Web Service for FastAPI and the compiled React/Vite assets, with Neon Free PostgreSQL as the deployed database. Render supports Python web services; Vite produces a static bundle and FastAPI can serve static files. **Inference:** serving that bundle and relative API routes from the same HTTPS origin preserves the React architecture and needs no cross-origin CORS configuration in this deployment. It does not require a second frontend deployment. [Render FastAPI guide](https://render.com/docs/deploy-fastapi), [Vite build](https://vite.dev/guide/build.html), [FastAPI static files](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/static-files/), [FastAPI origins](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/cors/).

Neon's 100 CU-hours means 400 active hours at 0.25 CU, rather than continuous operation throughout a month; larger compute consumes the allowance faster. Suspended compute consumes no CU-hours. [Plans](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/plans). Neon says activation typically takes a few hundred milliseconds; network latency and other connection work add time, so that is not a whole-application cold-start guarantee. [Connection latency](https://neon.com/docs/connect/connection-latency).

Render Free has an ephemeral filesystem and cannot attach a persistent disk. It may restart at any time. It may also suspend unusually high service-initiated external traffic, including external database/API traffic; no numerical threshold is published on that page. The Neon connection therefore has this additional availability caveat. Render Free PostgreSQL expires after 30 days and is **excluded** from this proposal. [Free instances](https://render.com/docs/free). Durable application records must reside in PostgreSQL rather than local SQLite or process memory; this is an architectural consequence of those restart/persistence limits.

## Keeping the bill at zero

Keep Render on Free/Hobby **without a payment method**, and Neon on Free without upgrading. Render explicitly warns that even Free instances can incur bandwidth/build charges when a payment method is present; without one it disables services instead. A pipeline spend limit covers builds, not a general bandwidth spending cap. [Render billing FAQ](https://render.com/docs/faq), [Free limits](https://render.com/docs/free). Neon advertises no-card Free access and documents quota suspension/failure rather than automatic paid overages on Free. [Neon pricing](https://neon.com/pricing), [Neon quota behaviour](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/plans).

Thus the expected hosting bill is **$0 while these free plans remain selected**, with downtime or blocked writes/builds at limits. This does not promise free external LLM usage or a purchased domain. The earlier $7.25 Render-with-disk recommendation does not meet the budget. No claim is made that every other free host lacks disks.

## Suitability and unresolved verification

Render says **“Do not use them for production applications.”** Its Free tier is positioned for testing/hobby/preview use. [Free-instance guidance](https://render.com/docs/free). This supports a course demonstration deployment; it does not support calling this a reliable production service. Neon similarly positions Free for prototypes, side projects and small teams, with no uptime SLA. [Neon plans](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/plans).

An additional official-domain search recovered the substantive [Render Terms](https://render.com/terms) (last modified 10 July 2026) and [Acceptable Use Policy](https://render.com/acceptable-use) (last modified 22 August 2025); ordinary page-open initially returned only the navigation shell. The Terms require a binding contract and compliance with applicable law, incorporate the AUP, and prohibit access to Render's Service by anyone under 16. That provider-account eligibility provision should not silently be converted into this app's own admission policy.

The AUP prohibits unlawful, harmful or objectionable content, child sexual exploitation, privacy infringements or collection without proper consent, unsolicited communications, impersonation, infrastructure abuse and unauthorized commercial purposes. No specific ban on educational adult dating/chat applications, or blanket prohibition of all commercial use on Free, was found in the retrieved official text. [AUP](https://render.com/acceptable-use). **Inference:** the described invite-only adult course demonstration is not categorically excluded merely because it involves dating/chat; actual content, consent and operation must still comply. This is neither Render's approval of this app nor legal certification. The separate free-tier production warning remains relevant regardless of AUP eligibility.

Recommendation for approval: retain this combination as a **$0 course-demo candidate**, accepting cold starts and quota outages, with Neon PostgreSQL as deployed storage. The plans are still available, so no replacement paid host or unsupported free-host alternative is proposed. No host has been selected or provisioned by this research.
